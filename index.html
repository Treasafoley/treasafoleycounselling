Welcome

My name is Treasa and I’m an integrative counsellor based In Exeter. I provide counselling online and in person for anyone aged 16+. I started studying person centered counselling back in 2019 and qualified as an integrative counsellor in 2023.

I work with anxiety (e.g. grief, self esteem, social anxiety), identity (e.g. sexuality, societal marginalisation) and relationship issues. (e.g. attachment, emotional abuse). My approach is collaborative; working with each client’s preferences and what feels most useful for them. Working intersectionally, I focus on how different identities shape your experience. In addition to my role as a counsellor, I also work in an educational setting and am keenly aware of the stressors that students and young people face, including barriers faced due to neurodiversity.

I am passionate about community and connection and my goal as a counsellor is to help people feel more anchored in a complex world that often fosters disconnection and isolation. Reaching out for support can feel daunting, but you don’t have to face things alone. If you’d like to explore working together, please send an enquiry below to arrange a free 15 minute telephone consultation.

Shorter sessions are available at a lower cost as a full 50 minute session does not work for everyone.
